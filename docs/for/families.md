# BnBDetector for Families

Families feel short-term rental buildings in a specific way: unfamiliar adults in the places children use, bedtimes broken by late arrivals, and neighbors who will not be there next week. BnBDetector does not score “family friendliness” as a lifestyle label. It scores nearby Airbnb activity, from 0 to 100, so you can see that pressure before you sign or buy.

Prefer Local Haven and Residential Core, scores 0–30, when you have a choice. Mixed Community, 31–58, deserves a real conversation: more guest traffic, less stable neighbors. Airbnb Dominated, 59–100, works against what most families are trying to buy with a long lease, which is a predictable building.

## What the score is a proxy for

A higher nearby listing count is a proxy for turnover. Turnover is the part that touches safety and routine: access details shared with a rotating set of guests, common areas used by people who do not live there, and noise that does not follow a neighbor’s weekday pattern. The report does not measure any of those directly. It measures detected Airbnb listings in a small area, about 80 meters. Read it as pressure on the address, then confirm with a visit.

Look at the entrance, the lift, the courtyard, and the play space, not only the bedroom. Go when guests are likely to move, if you can. A quiet Tuesday does not cancel a high score. A score does not cancel what you saw.

## A family shortlist

1. Filter first for the non-negotiables: school, space, budget, ground floor or lift.
2. Run BnBDetector on each address that survives. Do not spend the reports on maybes you would reject for size.
3. Keep 0–30 at the top. If every option in budget is 31–58, choose the lowest count and visit those buildings on purpose.
4. Read the building rules on short stays and guests. Ask who actually enforces them. A written rule is not the same as a quiet hallway, and the report is how you see the gap.
5. ReCheck the final address on Max or Ultra before you pay. A search that takes a season should not rely on the first week’s score.

The same report is included on every plan. Starter’s 10 one-time reports fit one move. Max fits a longer search and adds history and reCheck. Prices: https://www.bnbdetector.com/en/pricing

## What not to conclude

A low score is not a certificate that the building is safe, that the other residents are kind, or that the street is calm. A high score is not a finding you can hand to a school or a court as proof of a specific illegal unit. It is a dated count and a band. Quote it that way if you share it with a partner or a parent who is helping you decide.
